## EShot v4.2.4

### Linux recording

- Stabilized simultaneous desktop and microphone audio recording on PipeWire and EasyEffects setups
- Normalized both audio inputs to 48 kHz stereo before mixing to avoid repeating dropouts and mismatched source formats
- Added bounded queues, source timestamps, and resilient mixer settings for live audio sources
- Kept the recording pipeline compatible with the GStreamer version used by supported Linux builds
