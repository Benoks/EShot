## EShot v4.7.0

### Recording overhaul

- **Windows:** recordings now prefer Desktop Duplication through FFmpeg, with GDI capture as a fallback. Hardware H.264 encoding is used when supported and enabled.
- **Wayland:** rebuilt portal recording startup, region cropping, pause/resume segments and finalization. Hardware H.264 encoding supports compatible VA-API and NVIDIA encoders, with a software fallback.
- Windows GIF recording now uses an intermediate video and FFmpeg palette conversion, keeping encoding work off the interface thread.
- Fixed Wayland recordings stopping on static screens when the portal sends frames without timestamps.
- Improved microphone selection, desktop audio capture, device discovery and audio synchronization. Recording errors and missing dependencies are reported more clearly.

### Recording controls and settings

- One toolbar recording button opens GIF and video options, with Small, Balanced, High and editable Custom quality settings.
- When a tray icon is available, pause, resume, stop and cancel are controlled from its menu. The recording frame can be hidden or shown without interrupting the recording. A control bar remains available on desktops without tray support.
- The tray shows recording status and elapsed time, then switches to the saving/conversion state while the file is finalized.
- Canceling a recording now clears its tray controls reliably, including cancellation during startup. Late encoder progress cannot restart canceled recording controls.
- Recording shortcuts are registered only while needed. Saved-recording notifications work more reliably on KDE Plasma.

### Setup and updates

- The welcome screen fits its content instead of filling the screen, scrolls on small displays and keeps the Finish button reachable.
- First-run setup starts in English on Windows and Linux; the language selected when completing setup is saved for the application.
- **Managed portable installations (#40):** an empty `.portable` file beside the executable disables automatic update checks and silent updates. For AppImages, put it beside the original `.AppImage` file. Manual checks remain available, and settings storage is unchanged.

Windows x64, Windows ARM64 and Linux x86_64 packages are provided below. KDE Plasma 6 Wayland and GNOME Wayland remain the supported Linux desktop targets.
