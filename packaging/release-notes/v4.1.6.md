## EShot v4.1.6

### Annotation

- Added free rotation for selected text, arrows, lines, rectangles, circles, and semi-transparent shapes
- Added an on-canvas rotation handle with 15-degree Shift snapping
- Preserved rotation through moving, undo and redo

### Recording

- Added focused GIF and video setup panels after selecting a capture area
- Added video FPS, duration, quality, desktop-audio, microphone, and microphone-device controls
- Centers the setup panel on the monitor containing the selected area
- Added a lockable recording border and a tray action to cancel an active recording
- Refined compact number and device controls for a more consistent toolbar style

### Linux

- Improved the local KDE Plasma recording workflow while preserving the existing portal permission flow
