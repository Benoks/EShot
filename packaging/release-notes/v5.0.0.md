## EShot v5.0.0

### A new look

- **New design language** across the capture overlay and all windows: a warm charcoal theme, a light neutral accent instead of blue, flat 4 px controls and one set of colours shared by everything.
- **Capture toolbar in three bars:** drawing tools, colour and editing, sharing. Buttons have a subtle tile at rest and a clear selected state.
- **Recording bar:** the recording settings are now one row under the selection, like the toolbar, instead of a panel in the middle of the screen. Less used settings (frame rate, microphone, custom quality) open from "⋯".
- **Shortcut sheet (?):** one card per section, readable keycaps and balanced columns.
- Settings, OCR and upload windows use the same buttons, fields, checkboxes and tabs; every clickable control shows a pointing hand.

### Settings and first run

- **Search in Settings:** press Ctrl+F and type. Matching options are highlighted, each tab shows how many it has, and Enter jumps to the next one. Accents and case don't matter, and English words find the option in any language.
- **Start at login** is offered in the first-run wizard and checked by default, so Print Screen keeps working after a restart. The Windows installer's startup option is now checked by default too.
- The first-run wizard also lets you choose how the selection behaves: Lightshot-style (a click outside starts over) or locked (double-click outside to start over).

### Annotations

- **Resize shapes after drawing** (#47): select a rectangle, ellipse, blur or pixelate area and drag its corner or edge handles; drag the ends of lines and arrows or the tip of a counter's pointer. Shift keeps the proportions. Works on rotated shapes and can be undone.
- **Shift** now snaps lines and arrows to 45° steps and turns a pen stroke into a straight line, as the shortcut sheet always said.
- **Alt + drag** draws a rectangle or ellipse from its centre.
- **Ctrl+D** duplicates the selected object; a copied counter continues the numbering.
- **Fill shapes** option in Quick Settings for solid rectangles and ellipses.
- The width and blur sliders also change the selected object, one undo step per drag. A hint explains this the first times you move a slider with nothing selected.

### Capturing

- **Alt+Tab works as expected after a capture:** the window that was active before gets the focus back (KDE Plasma and Windows).
- **Right-click steps back:** it closes an open panel, puts the drawing tool away, deselects the object, and only then clears the selection.
- The microphone list in the recording bar can always be changed; picking a device turns the microphone on.
- Normal arrow cursor on the recording and settings panels instead of the move cursor.

### Uploads

- **One step:** the upload starts as soon as you click Upload, and the link is copied when it's done.
- **Upload history:** the last 50 links, with copy, open and remove, in the upload window.
- **Google Drive** uses resumable uploads, so screenshots larger than 5 MB upload too.
- TmpFiles (1 hour) and temp.sh (3 days, download page) show how long links last.
- Radikal Cloud is removed; its service is gone. If it was selected, Catbox is used.

### Faster

- Drawing, moving and resizing an object repaints only the area around it instead of the whole screen, which matters most on large and high-DPI displays.
- Text labels are no longer laid out again on every frame, and a copied or saved capture is composed once instead of twice.
- Less copying of whole screens on KDE, a lighter toolbar update while the selection is dragged, and smoother resizing of pinned images.
- On Linux X11, global hotkeys no longer wake EShot every 40 ms.

### Fixes

- A recording that is still being saved is no longer lost when EShot quits, updates or is closed by Setup; Setup and the uninstaller wait for it.
- The Linux updater restarts EShot if an update fails partway.
- A selection or box dragged up or to the left no longer ends up two pixels smaller.
- Nudging and right-click work right after drawing a region.
- Erasing, undoing or redoing while editing a label or holding a shape no longer moves the edit to another object.
- Quick Settings shows the selected shape's own fill.
- Resetting Settings applies the default theme, and exported empty hotkeys clear the field on import.
- Opening recording settings no longer brings back an old capture if a new one started meanwhile.
- Tooltips and hints follow a language change.
- Temporary upload images are removed after a cancelled upload.
