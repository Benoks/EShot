## EShot v4.6.1

### Counter tool (#38)

- **Drag to add a pointer:** click places a numbered counter as before, click and drag places it with an arrow pointing to where you release. The pointer moves, undoes and erases together with its counter
- **Start at:** choose the first number in Quick Settings while the Counter tool is active. It is remembered, so a tutorial spread over several screenshots can continue at 5, 6, 7…
- **Counter size:** new slider in Quick Settings; the pointer uses the normal width slider like the Arrow tool. Already placed counters keep their size
- The width slider is now remembered between sessions

### Selection (#39)

- **Arrow keys** move the finished selection by 1 px, **Shift+Arrow** by 10 px
- **Ctrl+Arrow** resizes the selection from its right/bottom edge (Ctrl+Shift: 10 px). This also works while the selection is locked
- With an object selected, the arrow keys nudge that object instead
- **Lock the selection automatically:** new option in Settings → Capture and in Quick Settings (off by default), so a click outside no longer loses the region. K still unlocks it
- The shortcut sheet (?) lists the new keys

Pinned images: Shift + mouse wheel resizes them, the plain wheel changes opacity.
